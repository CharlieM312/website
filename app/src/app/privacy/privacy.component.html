Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how charlie-meader.com ("Charlie Meader Airport Website & Tracker") collects, uses, and protects information when you browse the website, sign in, or log airport visits.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & OAuth Identity: When you sign in or link an account using GitHub, Google, or Microsoft OAuth, we receive your basic profile identifier (unique provider ID, display name/username, and email address) solely to identify your account.
  • Two-Factor Authentication & Passkeys: If you enable 2FA or register a YubiKey/Passkey (WebAuthn), we store the TOTP shared secret or the public key and credential ID required to verify your sign-in.
  • Airport Visits & Flight Data: When you log a visit on /tracker, we store the airport IATA codes, visit date, optional flight number, and optional notes associated with your username.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use a functional cookie (darkMode) to remember your theme preference and browser localStorage to store your active session token when signed in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and enforce administrator approval for write access.
  • To display logged airport visits, routes, and aggregated travel statistics on the website.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal data or OAuth profile details with advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Storage & Your Controls

Account and visit data are stored securely in Cloudflare D1. Signed-in users can rename their username, unlink OAuth providers, remove registered passkeys, disable 2FA, or edit and delete their logged airport visits at any time from the Tracker page.
